QADT AeroSAE 2027 Software Repository

Welcome! You've landed in the software home of Queen's Aerospace Design Team (QADT)'s entry for AeroSAE 2027 — drone autonomy, computer vision, hardware control, and the containers that tie it all together. Whether you're here for your first commit or your fiftieth, glad to have you.

This page is short on purpose — it's just here to point you the right way:

Setting up your dev environment?

→ SETUP.md walks you through everything, OS by OS: installing prerequisites, cloning the repo, getting the devcontainer running, and (if you make it that far) flying the drone in simulation.

Ready to contribute?

→ CONTRIBUTING.md covers the workflow — branching, PRs, review, and what to check before you touch a Dockerfile or the ROS workspace. main is protected, so every change lands through it.

Looking for what to work on?

→ The project board tracks tasks — what's up for grabs, what's in progress, and what's done.

Stuck, or looking for something else?

→ The GitHub Wiki is where team resources and common sources of pain live — onboarding slides, learning material, and the fixes people reach for most often. If you hit something that isn't there yet, add it once you've solved it so the next person doesn't have to re-solve it.

Questions that aren't answered by any of the above? Reach out to a manager!
